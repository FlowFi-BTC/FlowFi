**First, about the fear of people stealing your idea:**

This is very common. Here’s the reality:

- Most people will not steal it.
- Even if someone likes the idea, execution is what matters (especially the real-world relationships, trust, and follow-through).
- You already have smart contracts, a frontend, and documentation — that is already a head start.
- For a small pilot, the bigger risk is **not finding counterparties**, not someone copying you.

You can still protect yourself by:
- Not sharing full contract details or unique implementation tricks publicly
- Speaking at a high level
- Focusing the post on looking for pilot partners, not teaching people how to rebuild everything
